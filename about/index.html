About Doodle Hearth

Doodle Hearth is a small family studio that makes coloring books and printable coloring pages. The name says what we are after: the feeling of sitting by a warm hearth with a box of pencils and nowhere to be.

Who our pages are for

Kids get big, friendly pictures with thick outlines that small hands can manage. Teens and adults get cozy, bold-and-easy scenes that relax rather than frustrate. Seniors get extra-bold, large-print pages with one clear subject each, good for low vision and for memory care.

How the pages are made

We plan every page ourselves and draw them with the help of AI illustration tools. We are open about that. Then every picture is cleaned up to solid black lines and checked for closed shapes, stray gray and areas too small to color before it goes into a book or a printable.

Our printed books carry one picture per sheet with a blank back, so markers and gel pens do not ruin the next page.
