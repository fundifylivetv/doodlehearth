Privacy policy

Last updated: October 10, 2026

This website is a simple information site. It does not use advertising trackers, it does not set its own cookies, and it does not ask you for any personal information.

Fonts

Our text fonts load from Google Fonts, which means your browser contacts Google's servers when you open a page. Google's own privacy policy covers that request.

Buying from us

When you buy a printable or a book, the purchase happens on Payhip (card payments processed by Stripe), Etsy or Amazon. Those services handle your payment and delivery details under their own privacy policies. We receive only what they share with sellers to fulfil your order, such as your name and email address for a digital download, and we use it only for that order and for support.

Email

If you email us, we use your message and address only to reply. We never sell or share your information.

Children

Many of our coloring pages are made for children, but this website is written for the grown-ups who buy them. We do not knowingly collect any information from children.

Changes and contact

If we add a newsletter or analytics, we will update this page first. Questions: doodlehearth@gmail.com.
